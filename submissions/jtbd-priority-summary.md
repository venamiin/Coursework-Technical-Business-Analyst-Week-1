# JTBD and Prioritisation

## Step 1: Group the Evidence

The stakeholder interview notes were grouped into six recurring themes based on repeated observations across different stakeholders. The themes are:

1. Duplicate work
2. Missed follow-up
3. Poor visibility and data quality
4. Customer friction
5. Financial credibility
6. Change resistance and adoption
