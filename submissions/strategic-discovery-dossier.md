# Discovery Brief Template

## 1. Problem summary
Legacy Trust’s debt recovery process relies on spreadsheets, email trails, and a 20-year-old collections database to manage over 100,000 delinquent accounts. As volumes have grown, this fragmented approach has created duplicated activity, missed follow-ups, inconsistent status updates, and significant manual reconciliation. Agents spend valuable time on administrative tasks instead of accounts requiring human judgement, reducing operational capacity and creating inconsistent customer journeys. These issues are estimated to contribute to a 15% revenue loss, while inefficient processes increase operational costs. Leadership therefore needs evidence showing where the current workflow fails, which activities are suitable for automation, and whether a Phase 1 self-service portal can deliver a credible 12-month financial return.
