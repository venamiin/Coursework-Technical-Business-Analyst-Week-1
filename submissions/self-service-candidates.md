# Self-Service Candidates

## Selection Criteria

Potential self-service opportunities were assessed against four criteria:

- **High-volume:** The activity occurs frequently across delinquent accounts.
- **Repeatable:** The activity follows a consistent process.
- **Rules-driven:** The activity can be completed using clear rules rather than specialist judgement.
- **Lower-risk:** The activity does not normally require complex assessment, vulnerability judgement or specialist intervention.

The strongest candidates are straightforward customer and recovery activities that can reduce unnecessary agent handling while leaving complex cases with agents.

## Recommended Phase 1 Candidates

The strongest initial candidates are:

1. **AUTO-01 — View outstanding balance and account position**
2. **AUTO-02 — View available payment and resolution options**
3. **AUTO-03 — Make a payment through self-service**
4. **AUTO-04 — Set or manage a straightforward follow-up action**
5. **AUTO-06 — Capture straightforward contact/outcome information**

These opportunities focus Phase 1 on high-volume, repeatable activities while avoiding the assumption that every debt recovery case can be automated.

## Activities That Should Remain Agent-Led

Not every broken step should become a portal feature. The following activities should remain agent-led or require specialist involvement:

| Activity | Why it should remain agent-led |
|---|---|
| **Dispute / disputed balance** | Requires investigation and potentially judgement rather than a simple rules-based journey. |
| **Hardship cases** | May require consideration of individual circumstances and specialist handling. |
| **Complex cases requiring specialist handling** | The current process explicitly requires escalation to a team leader or specialist. |
| **Cases requiring further information or rework** | These cannot safely follow a simple automated path until the missing information or issue has been resolved. |
| **Unclear recovery outcomes** | The appropriate next action may require human judgement. |
| **Specialist escalation** | The process includes team leader/specialist review and should not be removed from the workflow simply to increase automation. |

The principle for Phase 1 is therefore:

**Automate straightforward, repeatable recovery activities while keeping complex, disputed, hardship and specialist cases agent-led.**
